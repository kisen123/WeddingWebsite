# Design

- `inbox/`: drop sketches here: photos of paper drawings, Excalidraw exports
  (https://excalidraw.com, free, no account), screenshots of sites you like.
  Name them so they say what they are, e.g. `2026-11-rsvp-idea-kaja.jpg`.
- `reviews.md`: log of design feedback rounds and their decisions.

Ask Claude: "run a design review on <topic>" to turn sketches into
variants on a private page that can be commented on.
